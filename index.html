He should never be our senator.” Hamilton has denied covering up Moore’s sexual misconduct — but told the Kansas Reflector in July he “absolutely” would handle things differently today. “This is a desperate and shameful attack from a losing politician on his own state’s largest Church. These smears from Roger Marshall were already examined in the primary by local reporters who found the church reported the incidents to law enforcement, and Adam later personally worked to help get this predator convicted,” said Tyson Brody, a spokesman for the Hamilton campaign. “Marshall is resorting to these desperate lies and false attacks because Kansans have learned Roger Marshall had pregnant mothers arrested and jailed, and voted to block the release of the Epstein files. Roger Marshall is desperate to hide from his record of protecting predators, and preying on low-income families in Kansas.” Current surveys show Marshall leading Hamilton by 1.5 percentage points ahead of the Nov. 3 election, according to the RealClearPolitics average. Moore was convicted in 2013 of misdemeanor sexual battery for inappropriately touching his son’s friend at a sleepover in 2010.
