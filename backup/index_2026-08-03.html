Longtime Republican Congressional Stalwart Dead At 83

Former Rep. Kay Granger (R-TX), the first Republican woman elected to Congress from Texas and the first Republican woman to chair the House Appropriations Committee, has died at the age of 83. Granger represented Texas’ 12th Congressional District for nearly three decades after first serving as mayor of Fort Worth, where she became the city’s first female mayor. During her 28 years in Congress, she rose to chair the powerful House Appropriations Committee, overseeing federal spending and becoming one of the most influential Republicans on Capitol Hill. House Speaker Mike Johnson (R-LA) paid tribute to Granger on Sunday evening, calling her a “dear friend” and praising her lifetime of public service.
