Making Mevis the scapegoat is the easy way out, but the harder thing to do is to look in the mirror and find ways you could have been better. Stafford delivered a revealing assessment on Thursday that should serve as a wake-up call for an offense talented enough to score on anybody, yet maddeningly inconsistent when it comes to opportunities in the game to put their opponents away. The problem isn’t necessarily how the Rams finish games or what the offense does when its backs are against the wall and it needs a score. They’ve been excellent when urgency is required, especially in two-minute situations. It’s more about the opportunities they miss before they reach the finish line.
