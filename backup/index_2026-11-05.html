Post-Election Data: Immigration Is A Winning Issue For Republicans

Amid Republicans’ many losses in the 2026 midterms is a silver lining indicating that the party still holds an advantage over Democrats on a key policy issue: immigration. NBC News exit polling suggests that, despite Democrat-led opposition to immigration enforcement and deportations, the GOP is generally viewed as more trustworthy on the topic. When asked how important immigration policies were in determining their vote for Congress, 73 percent said it was “crucial or a major factor,” while 23 percent said it was “a minor factor or not a factor.” Among the 73 percent who indicated the former, Republicans won 51 percent to 47 percent. Upon further analysis, the same poll found that, among the 36 percent who said the issue was “crucial” to their vote, Republicans won 57 to 42 percent. Among the 64 percent who said the issue was “a major factor or a minor factor or not a factor,” Democrats won 63 percent to 36 percent. The poll did find a majority of voters (60 to 40 percent) disapprove of the way ICE “is handling its job,” however. Data from CNN’s exit polling similarly appears to indicate that immigration enforcement remains a winning issue for Republicans. Of the 73 percent of respondents said such policy was a “crucial” or a “major factor” to their vote, Republicans won 51 to 47 percent. Among the 36 percent who said immigration enforcement was “crucial” to their vote, Republicans won 57 to 42 percent. Here is a quadrant chart using CNN’s exit poll. When it came to the general election, however, “ICE” plummeted to the 35th policy issue talked about in Democrat ads. The party’s general election ads instead focused heavily on health care and inflation, among other issues.
