Bosa got all the way to “soccer cheering techniques” in the course of politely asking a stadium full of people if they’d mind doing him a favor. And you can understand where he’s coming from. A good chant at a soccer match can survive a dozen changes of possession. Imagine having to stop singing every time somebody intercepted a pass. By the time you got going again, you’d have forgotten which verse you were on. Possession is a considerably bigger deal to a guy who makes his living chasing quarterbacks. If an offensive tackle has to turn his head to see the ball being snapped because he can’t hear the cadence, Bosa would gladly take whatever fraction of a second that buys him coming around the edge.
